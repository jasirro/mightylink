# MightyLink Companion - Browser Extension (Manifest V3)

MightyLink Companion allows you to save any webpage or link directly to your **MightyLink** workspace with a single click or keyboard shortcut.

---

## 🚀 Features

- **1-Click Bookmark Saving**: Automatically captures page title, clean URL, domain, and favicon.
- **Workspace Organizer**: Select which MightyLink workspace (Design System, Development, Marketing, etc.) to store the bookmark into.
- **Productivity Badges**:
  - ⭐ **Favorite**: Mark as a pinned favorite.
  - ⏰ **Todo / Reminder**: Queue the bookmark for follow-up review.
  - 🛡️ **Vault**: Store privately under encrypted lock.
- **Keyboard Shortcuts**:
  - `Alt + S` (or `Option + S` on Mac): Open the MightyLink popup.
  - `Ctrl + Shift + S` (or `Cmd + Shift + S` on Mac): Instant quick-save in the background without opening the popup.
- **Context Menus**: Right-click on any webpage or hyperlink and select *"Save page to MightyLink"* or *"Save this link to MightyLink"*.
- **Email Account Sync**: Sign in with your MightyLink account email for automatic workspace and link sync across devices.
- **Pairing Key Backup**: Connect via companion pairing token (`ML-8942-SYNC`) if desired.

---

## 📦 How to Install (Chrome, Brave, Edge, Opera)

1. Open your Chromium-based browser (Google Chrome, Brave, Microsoft Edge, Arc, etc.).
2. Navigate to your browser's extensions page:
   - **Chrome / Brave**: `chrome://extensions`
   - **Microsoft Edge**: `edge://extensions`
3. Toggle on **"Developer mode"** in the top-right corner.
4. Click the **"Load unpacked"** button in the top-left toolbar.
5. Select this directory:
   ```
   c:\Users\Administrator\Desktop\my project\linku\extension
   ```
6. The **MightyLink Companion** icon with the peering mascot will now appear in your browser toolbar!
7. *(Recommended)* Click the puzzle piece icon on your browser toolbar and **pin** MightyLink for instant 1-click access.

---

## 📧 Connecting via Your Email Account

1. Click the **MightyLink** extension icon on your browser toolbar.
2. Enter the same **email address** you use in your MightyLink mobile app (e.g. `alex@mightylink.io`).
3. Click **Sign In & Sync**.
4. The extension automatically loads your workspaces and syncs all bookmarks directly into your MightyLink account!
